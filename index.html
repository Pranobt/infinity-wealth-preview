Questions you might have.

Not here? Ask us on a call.

What is Infinity?

We help Indian families and NRIs plan, invest and review their money.

But what exactly do you do?

We look at your whole money picture, build a plan with you, and keep reviewing it as things change.

Do you work with people outside India?

Yes. We work with families in India and abroad, across time zones.

Is my information safe with you?

Yes. Confidentiality is the core of how we work. Your details are used for your plan and nothing else.

Why pay for advice when I could invest on my own?

You can invest on your own. What we add is the whole picture: cash flow, tax, loans, cards and protection planned together, and someone who keeps checking it as your life changes.

Can you look at investments I already have?

Yes. That is part of the discovery call. We go through what you already own before we suggest anything new.

How are you different from an investing app?

An app can pick funds. It cannot sit with you through a home loan decision, a move abroad or a bad year in the markets. We plan the whole picture, and a real person stays with you.

How often will we review my plan?

Regularly, and whenever something big changes, like a new job, a move or a new child. We agree the rhythm with you on the walkthrough call.

Do we meet online or in person?

Our calls happen online, so it works wherever you live, in India or abroad.
